Data exploration (not graded)

Scratch page for looking at the BuzzFeed gentrification data before we design the real charts. Only tracts that were eligible to gentrify in 2000 (low income + low home value) are used, so "gentrified" vs "not gentrified" compares similar neighborhoods.

1. Change in Black population share, 2000 to 2017

Average percentage-point change per tract. Bars below 0 = Black share went down.

2. Who moved in? Change in each group's share in gentrified tracts

Each dot is the average percentage-point change for that group, gentrified tracts only.

3. Home value growth vs change in Black share (every eligible tract)

One dot per tract. Home value change is inflation-adjusted (BuzzFeed's home_pct_change). A few extreme tracts are cut off.
